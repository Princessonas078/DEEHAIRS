Our Story

Meet Dee

Dee is a passionate, highly skilled hair braiding artist based in the UK with years of experience creating flawless, long-lasting styles that turn heads and boost confidence. What sets Dee apart is her meticulous attention to detail, a gentle technique that always prioritises hair health, and her extraordinary ability to bring each client's unique vision to life.

Whether you're after a sleek, low-maintenance everyday look or a jaw-dropping statement style for a special occasion, Dee's signature touch delivers every time. Clients travel from across the city — and beyond — just to sit in her chair.

When you book with HAIRBYDEE Studio, you're not just getting your hair done. You're getting a full, elevated experience — one that leaves you feeling like the most beautiful version of yourself.

Booking Policy

  • 💳
    Deposit Required A £20 deposit is required to secure your appointment. This confirms your booking and is deducted from your final balance.
  • 🚫
    Non-Refundable Deposits Please note that deposits are strictly non-refundable. We kindly ask that you are certain of your appointment before booking.
  • ⏰
    Punctuality Please arrive on time for your appointment. We have a schedule to keep and want to give every client the attention they deserve.
  • ⚠️
    Late Arrivals Late arrivals may result in your appointment being cancelled or shortened. Please message us in advance if you're running behind.
  • 📵
    No-Call / No-Show Clients who do not show up or fail to cancel in advance will forfeit their deposit. Repeated no-shows may be refused future bookings.
